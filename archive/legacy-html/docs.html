PYRE is a DeFi protocol built on a single belief — that conviction should be rewarded permanently, and indifference should have a cost. Everything that follows is a consequence of that idea.

01

What PYRE is

Most tokens are designed to reward holding. Buy, wait, sell when it's high enough. The protocol inflates, the team distributes, and everyone pretends the dilution isn't happening. PYRE was built on the belief that this model is dishonest.

PYRE is a token with built-in decay — liquid tokens lose value every hour. Stopping the decay requires commitment. Transcending it entirely requires burning. Both paths are rewarded. Neither is optional.

There are no VCs. No pre-mine. No team allocation on a vesting cliff. The protocol launched with equal access for everyone — the only advantage available is arriving before the price rises.

The core idea: Stake to survive the decay. Burn to transcend it entirely. Every token that burns becomes a permanent yield position, held by a Fire Spirit NFT that earns forever.

02

The Decay Mechanic

Every liquid $PYRE token — tokens sitting in a wallet, not staked, not burned — loses value at a fixed rate every hour. This is called decay. It is calculated and applied on-chain at every epoch (approximately one hour).

Decay is not punishment. It is an honest representation of what indifference costs. Every other token in the market also loses value over time through inflation and team distribution — PYRE simply says so out loud.

0.45% Decay per hour — Era 0
~57% Lost over 7-day drip exit
0.01% Floor rate — token survives forever

The decay rate halves every 2,000 epochs — roughly every 83 days. Each halving is called an Era. The rate declines progressively until it reaches a permanent floor of 0.01% per hour, at which point $PYRE survives indefinitely.

Exiting PYRE requires a 7-day drip — tokens unlock gradually over seven days. During this period, decay continues and no yield is earned. This creates a meaningful cost for indecision and rewards those who act with conviction.

03

Staking — Survive the Decay

Staking is the first path. When $PYRE is staked, decay stops completely. Staked tokens are protected — they do not lose value over time.

In return, stakers earn a proportional share of all ETH fees generated by every swap in the PYRE liquidity pool. Every buy and sell on the protocol produces ETH fees that flow continuously to stakers.

Staking does not create a visual identity or NFT. It is a purely financial commitment — decay protection and yield share. The Fire Spirit NFT belongs exclusively to those who burn.

There is no minimum stake and no lock period beyond the 7-day drip required to exit. Stakers can leave at any time — but the drip cost and loss of yield makes long-term commitment the rational choice.

04

Burning — Transcend It

Burning is the second path — and the permanent one. When $PYRE tokens are burned, they are destroyed forever. In return, the burner receives something that cannot be taken away: a Fire Spirit NFT that earns yield permanently.

Burning is irreversible. There is no unstaking, no exit drip. The decision is final. This permanence is the point — the protocol rewards it accordingly, with yield multipliers that increase as cumulative burns grow.

Burns accumulate across transactions. Every token burned by an address adds to that address's cumulative burn total, which determines their Fire Spirit's current stage. Partial burns are saved — nothing is lost.

Sell-side fees are burned too. Every sell on the protocol collects a fee in $PYRE — and 100% of that is permanently burned. Every transaction shrinks supply.

05

Fire Spirits

A Fire Spirit is an NFT minted automatically when a wallet's cumulative $PYRE burns cross 10,000 tokens. It is generative — the art is computed entirely on-chain from a seed locked at the moment of first mint. Every Fire Spirit's final form is determined at EMBER stage, revealed progressively as the wallet burns more.

Fire Spirits evolve through four stages. The art updates in place — same token ID, same NFT, new form. No metadata update transaction required. The art reflects the current stage automatically.

The yield multiplier reflects the NFT holder's share of the yield pool relative to other holders. A PYRE-stage Fire Spirit earns three times the yield weight of an EMBER-stage one. Higher stages are more exclusive as price rises — the same threshold costs more, rewarding those who committed earliest.

LP burners — wallets that burn ETH+PYRE liquidity positions rather than raw tokens — earn a +20% weight bonus on top of their stage multiplier, reflecting the deeper commitment of removing liquidity permanently.

Fire Spirit yield follows the NFT, not the wallet. When a Fire Spirit is transferred or sold, the accumulated yield settles to the seller automatically, and the new holder begins earning from that point forward.

06

Where Yield Comes From

PYRE generates yield from a single source: swap fees on every trade through the PYRE liquidity pool. There is no external protocol, no farming strategy, no external dependency. The yield is a direct function of trading volume.

5% Total effective fee per swap
80% Buy-side ETH → yield pool
100% Sell-side PYRE → burned
20% Buy-side ETH → team

Buy-side fees are collected in ETH. 80% flows to the yield pool, distributed proportionally between stakers and Fire Spirit holders by yield weight. 20% goes to the team. Sell-side fees are collected in $PYRE and burned permanently — every sell reduces supply.

Yield is distributed continuously — there is no epoch-based claim, no minimum threshold, no lockup on earned ETH. Stakers and burners earn in real time from every swap that occurs.

At $1M daily volume: the yield pool receives ~$43,200/day in ETH. ~$36,000/day in $PYRE is permanently burned. At $5M TVL staked, that represents ~315% ETH APY.

07

The Immolated

The Immolated is not a tier. It is a title — earned by the wallets that have committed most completely to the fire. It is the rarest identity in the PYRE protocol and it cannot be purchased, only earned.

To become Immolated, a wallet must reach PYRE stage (300,000 cumulative $PYRE burned) and then burn an additional 10,000 $PYRE on top of that. Both the LP path and the token path are eligible.

The Immolated gain access to a private area of the protocol — the Hall of the Immolated — and carry a visual identity that marks them permanently on the leaderboard and within the Village World.

The 10,000 additional burn mirrors the EMBER threshold intentionally — the entry point and the apex are the same number. Symmetry as design.

There is no hard cap on how many wallets can become Immolated. The threshold is fixed in tokens — as price rises, fewer wallets can reach it. Early participants who burned when price was low will always have an advantage that cannot be replicated later.
