PYRE Protocol

Technical reference for the PYRE protocol — a Uniswap V4 hook system on Ethereum mainnet. Every swap mints $PYRE. Every unstaked token burns at 0.45%/hr, halving every 2,000 epochs. Staking stops the burn and earns proportional ETH yield. Burn $PYRE to mint a Fire Spirit NFT and earn perpetual yield weighted by how much you've burned.

There is one NFT: the Fire Spirit. It is earned by burning, evolves through four stages based on cumulative burns, and carries your yield position on-chain. Fully tradeable — yield auto-settles on transfer. This document covers every mechanic, every contract, and every formula.

Status

Contracts are unaudited. Do not deploy to mainnet until audits complete.

How It Works

PYRE compresses the entire protocol into one inevitable loop:

  1. Buy $PYRE by swapping ETH through the V4 hook — the hook mints tokens proportional to your volume
  2. Your effective balance immediately begins decaying at 0.45%/hr — stake to stop it
  3. Stake to pause decay and earn proportional ETH yield from the fee pool
  4. Burn $PYRE to mint a Fire Spirit NFT — burns accumulate, NFT mints at 10,000 cumulative
  5. Burn more to evolve: 75k → FLAME (1.5×), 150k → FORGE (2×), 300k → PYRE (3×)
  6. Fire Spirit earns perpetual ETH yield proportional to your burn weight × stage multiplier, forever

Every 2,000 epochs (~83 days), the decay rate halves. Era 0 pressure gives way to Era 1 relief — the protocol rewards those who survived the fire.

Minting

$PYRE is minted exclusively by the V4 hook on every inbound ETH swap. The amount minted per swap decreases over time as S(t) decays — creating early-mover advantage without a hard cap on participation.

math
mintAmount = volume × BASE_RATE × S(t)

  volume    = |amountSpecified| from swap params (in wei)
  BASE_RATE = immutable constant set at deploy (e.g. 1e14)
  S(t)      = global scaling factor — starts at 1.0, decays toward floor

Example: BASE_RATE=1e14, S(t)=0.8, 1 ETH swap
  1e18 × 1e14 × 8e17 / 1e36 = 0.00008 $PYRE minted

Decay & Halving Schedule

Every unstaked token decays continuously. The rate starts at 0.45%/hr and halves every 2,000 epochs — each epoch is one hour, each halving is approximately 83 days. A floor of 0.01%/hr is enforced permanently.

math
effectiveBalance = rawBalance × (scalingFactor / 1e18)

scalingFactor updated once per epoch:
  newScaling = oldScaling × (10000 - currentRate) / 10000

Era rates:
  Era 0 (epochs    0–2,000):  0.450%/hr  →  ~10.4%/day
  Era 1 (epochs 2,000–4,000): 0.225%/hr  →  ~5.2%/day
  Era 2 (epochs 4,000–6,000): 0.113%/hr  →  ~2.6%/day
  Era 3 (epochs 6,000–8,000): 0.056%/hr  →  ~1.3%/day
  Era 4 (epochs 8,000–10,000):0.028%/hr  →  ~0.67%/day
  Era 5 (epoch 10,000+):      0.014%/hr  →  ~0.34%/day
  Floor:                      0.010%/hr  →  permanent

Survival rates at Era 0 (0.45%/hr):
  24 hours →  89.7% remains
  7 days   →  47.0% remains   (drip window — ~53% lost)
  30 days  →   3.9% remains
  90 days  →   0.0001% remains
Staked tokens are exempt

The scaling factor only applies to liquid and LP balances. Staked balances are stored as raw values and returned in full — zero decay while staked.

Global Scaling Factor S(t)

S(t) governs the mint rate over time — separate from the decay scaling factor above. As S(t) decreases, each ETH swap mints fewer $PYRE. This creates early-buyer advantage without a vesting cliff.

The Fire Spirit

There is one NFT in the PYRE protocol: the Fire Spirit. It is the only NFT. It is tradeable. It tells the complete story of a wallet's commitment on-chain.

A Fire Spirit mints when a wallet's cumulative $PYRE burns cross 10,000. Burns accumulate across every transaction — no burn is ever wasted. As cumulative burns cross higher thresholds the art evolves in place — same token ID, same wallet, changing appearance. No IPFS. Art generated entirely on-chain as SVG.

The Immolated Protocol — Overview

The Immolated Protocol is the deepest layer of commitment. The Immolated permanently remove $PYRE from supply and hold a perpetual claim on 25% of all swap fees — forever, regardless of whether they continue staking.

Immolated status is not a second NFT. It is a permanent mark fused directly onto the existing Fire Spirit. The Fire Spirit's on-chain SVG renders the Immolated overlay for all to see.

Token Burn Path

Burn a minimum of 10,000 $PYRE directly. Tokens are sent to address(0) — permanently removed from supply. Your Fire Spirit receives the Immolated mark.

solidity
// Weight = underlying units (pre-scaling) — early burners get more weight
uint256 weight = amount * 1e18 / scalingFactorWAD;

// e.g. burn 1000 $PYRE at S(t)=0.7:
//   weight = 1000e18 * 1e18 / 0.7e18 = 1,428 underlying units

Because weight is denominated in underlying units, burning 1,000 $PYRE at Era 0 creates more weight than burning 1,000 $PYRE at Era 3. Early Immolated are permanently and disproportionately rewarded for their conviction.

LP Burn Path

Deposit ETH + $PYRE into the liquidity pool and burn the resulting LP shares permanently. The liquidity is locked forever — deepening the protocol's permanent floor. LP Immolated receive a 20% weight bonus on top of their $PYRE-equivalent value, and their Fire Spirit receives a distinct gradient visual treatment.

// LP weight with bonus
uint256 baseWeight = pyreAmt * 1e18 / scalingFactorWAD;

Irreversible

ETH and $PYRE deposited into the pool via LP burn are locked permanently. The liquidity cannot be withdrawn. This is by design — LP Immolated are rewarded with higher weight precisely because they have committed deeper.

How to Stake

Staking is immediate — no warmup, no lockup. Staked balances are completely exempt from decay. Your Fire Spirit evolution clock advances only while staked.

  1. Approve $PYRE spending: pyreToken.approve(stakingContract, amount)
  2. Call stake(uint256 amount) — tokens transferred, decay paused instantly
  3. If this is your first stake and effective balance ≥ 200K, Fire Spirit auto-mints
  4. Yield accrues from the moment you stake — claim anytime with claimFees()
Top-up allowed

You can stake additional $PYRE at any time without resetting your evolution clock. Only unstaking pauses the clock — it does not reset it.

Fee Distribution

100% of swap fees generated by the PYRE pool flow to active participants. Zero protocol cut.

math
Swap fees (100%)
├── Stakers:       75%  → split by (stakedBalance × stageMultiplier)
└── the Immolated: 25%  → split by (immolatedWeight in underlying units)

Staker reward per token:
  rewardPerToken += (newStakerFees * 1e18) / totalWeightedStake

Effective weight per staker:
  weight = stakedBalance * stageMultiplier[stage]

Stage multipliers:
  EMBER (1): 1×   FLAME (2): 2×   FORGE (3): 4×   PYRE (4): 7×

Yield is denominated in ETH — not $PYRE. There is no inflationary APY. If swap volume is low, yield is low. The protocol's health is directly legible from fee distribution.

The Drip Exit

Unstaking does not return tokens immediately. It initiates a 7-day linear drip during which tokens continue decaying at the current epoch rate. The drip is the entire exit cost — there are no additional fees.

math
claimable = dripAmount × (elapsed / 7 days) × (scalingFactor / 1e18)

Era 0 drip loss (0.250%/hr × 168 hours):
  0.9975^168 = e^(168 × -0.002503) ≈ 0.656
  → ~34.4% of tokens lost over full 7-day drip

Era 1 drip loss (0.125%/hr × 168 hours):
  → ~19.6% of tokens lost (halvings make exit cheaper over time)
Clock behavior

Unstaking pauses your evolution clock — it does not reset it. Your accumulated staking time is preserved. Restaking resumes the clock from where it stopped. You cannot restake until the current drip is fully claimed.

PyreToken.sol

ERC-20 with global lazy decay. Staked balances skip the scaling factor entirely.

solidity
uint256 public epochCount;
uint256 public currentDecayRate = 250; // 0.250% = basis-tenth-points
uint256 public scalingFactorWAD = 1e18;

function balanceOf(address a) public view override returns (uint256) {
    if (isStaked[a]) return rawBalance[a];
    return rawBalance[a] * scalingFactorWAD / 1e18;
}

// Called by Chainlink Automation every 3600 seconds
function _tickEpoch() internal {
    epochCount++;
    uint256 era = epochCount / 2500;
    uint256 r   = 250 >> era;
    currentDecayRate = r < 5 ? 5 : r;
    scalingFactorWAD = scalingFactorWAD * (10000 - currentDecayRate) / 10000;
}

PyreNFT.sol

Tradeable ERC-721. One token per wallet. Visual stage + Immolated status rendered entirely on-chain. Yield settles on transfer via _beforeTokenTransfer.

solidity
function currentStage(address wallet) public view returns (uint8) {
    uint256 total = accumulatedStakingTime[wallet];
    if (stakeStart[wallet] != 0)
        total += block.timestamp - stakeStart[wallet];
    if (total >= 180 days) return 4; // PYRE
    if (total >= 90  days) return 3; // FORGE
    if (total >= 30  days) return 2; // FLAME
    return 1;                         // EMBER
}

function _beforeTokenTransfer(address from, address to, uint256 id) internal override {
    if (from != address(0)) {
        IPyreStaking(staking).settleYield(from, to, id);   // settle staker yield
        IPyreImmolated(forged).settleYield(from, id, id);  // settle immolated yield
        peakStage[id] = currentStage(from);                // lock visual stage
        accumulatedStakingTime[to] = 0;                    // new holder starts fresh
        stakeStart[to] = 0;
    }
}

PyreStaking.sol

Synthetix-style O(1) fee distribution. Stage multiplier applied at reward claim time.

solidity
uint256[5] public stageMultiplier = [0, 1e18, 2e18, 4e18, 7e18];

function stake(uint256 amount) external nonReentrant {
    _settleReward(msg.sender);
    IPyreToken(pyreToken).transferFrom(msg.sender, address(this), amount);
    IPyreToken(pyreToken).setStaked(msg.sender, true);
    positions[msg.sender].amount += amount;
    IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).startClock(msg.sender);
    totalWeightedStake += amount * stageMultiplier[_stage(msg.sender)] / 1e18;
}

function unstake(uint256 amount) external nonReentrant {
    _settleReward(msg.sender);
    positions[msg.sender].amount -= amount;
    IPyreToken(pyreToken).setStaked(msg.sender, false);
    IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).pauseClock(msg.sender); // pauses, does NOT reset
    drips[msg.sender] = Drip({ amount: amount, startTime: block.timestamp, claimed: 0 });
}

PyreImmolated.sol

Handles both burn paths, weight accounting, and perpetual 25% fee distribution.

solidity
uint256 public constant MIN_BURN        = 100e18;   // 100 $PYRE
uint256 public constant LP_WEIGHT_BONUS = 1_200;    // × 1.20
uint256 public constant BONUS_DENOM     = 1_000;

function burnTokens(uint256 amount) external nonReentrant {
    require(amount >= MIN_BURN);
    uint256 tokenId = IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).tokenOf(msg.sender);
    require(IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).currentStage(msg.sender) >= 3, "FORGE required");
    _settleReward(tokenId);
    uint256 weight = amount * 1e18 / IPyreToken(pyreToken).scalingFactorWAD();
    IPyreToken(pyreToken).transferFrom(msg.sender, address(0), amount);
    immolatedWeight[tokenId] += weight;
    totalImmolatedWeight      += weight;
    IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).setImmolated(tokenId, false);
}

function burnLP(uint256 ethAmt, uint256 pyreAmt) external payable nonReentrant {
    require(msg.value == ethAmt);
    uint256 tokenId  = IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).tokenOf(msg.sender);
    require(IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).currentStage(msg.sender) >= 3, "FORGE required");
    _settleReward(tokenId);
    uint256 lp       = _addLiquidity(ethAmt, pyreAmt);
    uint256 weight   = (pyreAmt * 1e18 / scalingFactorWAD()) * LP_WEIGHT_BONUS / BONUS_DENOM;
    IERC20(lpToken).transfer(address(0), lp); // burn LP permanently
    immolatedWeight[tokenId] += weight;
    totalImmolatedWeight      += weight;
    IPyreNFT(pyreNFT).setImmolated(tokenId, true); // true = LP variant
}
