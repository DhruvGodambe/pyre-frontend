Brief 01 — Manifesto Launch Graphic
2 deliverables: static PNG + short video
▶
Purpose
Appears on tweet 1 of the manifesto thread — the first thing anyone sees in a feed. Its only job is to stop the scroll and create a feeling. It does not explain PYRE. No mechanics, no numbers, no bullet points.
The line
Stake to survive.
Burn to transcend.
This is the only text on the graphic. Nothing else.
Visual Direction
Background: #080504 — full bleed, near black
Text font: Cormorant Garamond, Light (300)
Text colour: #C9A96E (PYRE gold)
Layout: Two lines, centred. Wide letter spacing — elegant, not cramped.
    "Stake to survive." sits above. "Burn to transcend." sits below.
    A real beat of space between them — two declarations, not one sentence.

Fire element: ONE controlled, elegant element. Not a raging bonfire. Options:
    — A single thin vertical flame, centred below the text
    — Rising embers drifting upward from the bottom edge
    — A subtle glow emanating from below, as if something burns underneath
The fire should feel restrained and intentional. Ember, not explosion.

Wordmark: Bottom centre, very small, same gold, wide letter spacing. Almost invisible — a signature.
Nothing else: No URL. No date. No ticker. No borders. Restraint is the point.
Deliverable 1 — Static
Format: PNG
Dimensions: 1600 × 900px (landscape, optimised for X feed)
Deliverable 2 — Video (3–4 seconds, silent)
Format: MP4 · 1600 × 900px · Silent — no audio

Sequence:
  1. Black screen, fire element present (subtle, slow)
  2. "Stake to survive." fades in — holds 1.5 seconds
  3. "Burn to transcend." fades in beneath — holds 1.5 seconds
  4. Both lines remain. Video ends.

Loop point: Clean — last frame matches first so X autoplay loops seamlessly.
End: No hard cut — the final frame should feel like it could hold forever.
Reference Feeling
Luxury fashion. High-end editorial. Dark, quiet, confident. A typical DeFi launch graphic = wrong. Perfume ad or art exhibition announcement = right.
No deadline set. Deliver when complete. Post date TBD — will be communicated separately.

Brief 02 — Fire Spirit Collection
4 stages × art direction — reveals sequentially
▶
What Fire Spirits are
Fire Spirits are on-chain NFTs minted automatically when a wallet burns 10,000+ PYRE tokens. They evolve through four stages as the wallet burns more. The art is generative but the direction is editorial — each stage has a distinct visual identity that reflects increasing commitment and power.
The Four Stages
StageThresholdArt DirectionFeel
EMBER 10,000 PYRE Silhouette form. Barely visible. Like something just beginning to glow — heat, not light. Form is human-ish but abstracted. Colour palette: deep coal greys, faint orange glow at edges. Nascent. Potential, not power.
FLAME 75,000 PYRE One trait revealed. The form clarifies — a face, hands, or some defining feature emerges from the silhouette. Amber and deep orange. More fire, more presence. Awakening. The commitment is showing.
FORGE 150,000 PYRE Two traits revealed. The Spirit is more defined — posture clear, character emerging. Colour: bright oranges and golds with darker underlighting. Sculpted, not burning. Becoming. Intentional. Identity formed.
PYRE 300,000 PYRE Full form revealed. Complete being. Majestic, controlled power. Not chaotic flame — contained fire. Colour: deep reds, charcoal, gold accent. The PYRE Spirit looks ancient and knowing. Transcended. Permanent. Irreducible.
Post 3 — First Reveal (EMBER Stage)
This is a social post — the visual does the work. Show ONE Fire Spirit at EMBER stage. Minimal text. The goal: people screenshot and share it without knowing what it is yet. The visual creates the question.
Format: Square 1080×1080px or 4:5 portrait for feed
Text: Minimal or none — if text, just "EMBER" in small Fira Code, bottom corner
Feel: Mysterious. The first glimpse. Not a reveal — a silhouette of a reveal.
Post 4 — The Four Stages
Show all four stages as a progression. This is educational — it needs to communicate the evolution from EMBER to PYRE visually, before anyone reads the caption.
Format: Landscape 1600×900px
Layout: Four stages side by side (left to right), each with name and threshold below in Fira Code
Background: #080504 — same as always
Feel: Progression, evolution, becoming. Like four chapters of the same story.
No deadline set. EMBER reveal before Four Stages. Post dates communicated when confirmed.

Brief 03 — Completion Share Card
1 deliverable — dynamic personalised card
▶
Context
After completing the pre-launch task funnel and submitting their wallet, users see a completion screen. The final action: they get a branded card copied to their clipboard to post on X alongside a pre-filled tweet. This card is the social proof moment — it needs to look like something people actually want to post. A flex, not a badge.
Card Content
Background: #080504 — near black, full bleed
Central element: A fire element — ember or flame, same style as manifesto graphic
Primary text: "The fire starts." — large, Cormorant Garamond, gold
Wallet line: Partial wallet address (first 6 chars + "..." + last 4 chars) — small, Fira Code, secondary gold
    Example: "0x1A3f...B7c2"
Wordmark: PYRE — small, bottom, same gold. Almost invisible.
Nothing else: No date. No mechanics. No website.
Design Goal
When someone posts this on X: it should look beautiful enough that people ask "what is this?" — not "what crypto badge is this?" People copy this because it looks good, not because they were told to.
Format
Size: 1200 × 675px (standard X link card size)
Format: PNG (will be copied to clipboard via JS)
Personalisation: The wallet address section will be composited by the frontend — deliver with a clear placeholder zone
No deadline set. Needed before task funnel goes live.
