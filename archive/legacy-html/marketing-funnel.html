The Funnel

Four steps. Nothing more.

1

Complete the tasks — no wallet needed

Everything runs in a regular browser. Follow PYRE on X, share posts as they go live, complete the mechanic quiz, refer one person. No extensions, no signing, no friction. Works on any phone, any browser.

// tasks tied to X posts unlock automatically on schedule — no manual activation
2

Submit your wallet address

Just a text field. Type your address, hit submit. No wallet connection, no MetaMask required. If you submit the wrong address you simply don't get the boost — it's self-correcting. Submission closes June 22.

3

Share your completion on X

After submitting, a completion screen appears with one button: "I'm ready to burn." Clicking it copies a branded image to your clipboard and opens a pre-filled tweet — one line, your call to the fire. Paste the image, post it. Can't be forced, but the people who do it are your most engaged users and their posts carry the most weight right before launch.

// image: dark card, fire aesthetic, partial wallet address, "The fire starts June 23." — designed to look like a flex, not a badge
4

Launch day — your whitelist position activates

CA drops on X at launch — fair launch, everyone sees it at the same time. Whitelisted wallets that stake early automatically receive their rewards. No claim needed — staking triggers it automatically.

KOL Strategy

Three waves. All disclosed.

⚠️

97% of crypto KOL campaigns don't disclose paid posts (ZachXBT / Memenetic data, Sep 2025). Make #ad disclosure contractual and announce the policy publicly before launch — it's a genuine differentiator in this market.

Wave 1 · Jun 7–12

Educators

3–5 niche DeFi accounts. Explainer content — the burn mechanic, not the price. Staggered, all disclosed. Drive early Village World traffic.

Wave 2 · Jun 13–19

Conviction

Mid-tier, larger audiences. Personal thesis: why they're watching PYRE. Drives curious → researching. Still disclosed and staggered.

Wave 3 · Jun 20–23

Countdown

All KOLs coordinated in 48-hour window. One burns live on-screen at launch. Focus: the mechanical reason to stake early, not price calls.

Wave 4 · Jun 30+

Authentic

KOLs share their Fire Spirit post-burn. On-chain authentic, disclosed. Most compelling — the transaction is publicly verifiable.

Contract Note

What dev needs to build for this

PyreStaking.sol — additions required
  • mapping(address => bool) public whitelist — populated from Village World wallet submissions before launch
  • uint256 public launchTimestamp — set at pool open, gates the boost claim window
  • Weight modifier: if whitelist[msg.sender] AND staked within launchTimestamp + 48h AND within launchTimestamp + 7 days → apply 1.2× yield weight
  • Boost expires automatically at launchTimestamp + 7 days — no manual action needed
  • One-time per wallet — no stacking, no transferring the whitelist slot
  • Village World backend must export the final wallet list and call a whitelist setter on the contract before launch
