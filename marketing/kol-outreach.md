## 1. What we offer (no fiat, ever)

All compensation is position in the protocol, structured so the KOL's upside
is the protocol's upside:

| Component | What it is | Why it converts them into supporters |
|---|---|---|
| Vested $PYRE allocation | From the marketing reserve, delivered **as a staked position** (force-staked at launch, releases linearly over the release window) | They earn $ETH yield from day one but cannot dump. Their income scales with protocol health, so they keep talking about it. |
| Early staking access | They may stake before the public pool opens | Costs us nothing, feels like status. Pairs with the force-stake. |
| Forge-tier Acolyte (select partners only, hard cap ~10) | Pre-minted second-highest tier (2× on the token track) | The free-ride that still demands the climb: Pyre tier needs 150,000 more burned by them, Immolation another 100,000. Their journey to the top IS Wave 4 content ("watch me burn to Pyre on stream"). |

Recommended internal anchors (finalize per partner, never in writing first):
- Allocation size: tier the allocations by reach and fit, not follower count alone; small enough that no single KOL matters, large enough to care. Suggested release: 3 to 6 months linear from launch.
- The Forge Acolyte is worth 150,000 $PYRE of burn. Treat it as the headline gift for the few partners we actually want long-term, not a default.
- Whitelist all partner wallets for the +20% quest boost like everyone else; no special public-facing perks.

**Never offered:** fiat, revenue share, "guaranteed" anything, price targets.

## 2. Hard rules (every message, every partner)

1. **Never name the yield source.** Staking "accrues $ETH yield". No tax, toll,
   fee, or transaction-cut language, in DMs too. If a KOL asks where yield
   comes from, point them to the Codex architecture chapter and let the
   protocol speak.
2. **No team-control talk.** The hook is "modular", never upgradeable/admin.
3. **No price talk, no return promises, no "investment" framing.** We offer a
   partnership paid in position, not a deal on future price.
4. **Style:** Pyre / Pyre Protocol (only P capital), $PYRE and $ETH always with
   the $, no em-dashes in anything a KOL will read.
5. **Privacy:** outreach only from the Pyre Protocol X account or project
   email. Never a personal account, never a personal name.
6. **Terms are private, the relationship is not.** Disclosure (#ad) is a
   contractual requirement (stated publicly before launch); the specific
   allocation/Acolyte terms are never public and never in screenshots.
7. **Vet before offering:** wallet history (no serial dumpers), audience
   quality (real engagement, not botted), past shills (rugs disqualify).

## 3. Process, A to Z

1. **Longlist** (ongoing): collect 30 to 50 candidates across three profiles:
   DeFi educators (Wave 1), conviction/narrative accounts (Wave 2), countdown
   amplifiers (Wave 3). Nano and mid-tier first; they reply and they need us.
2. **Vet** (§2.7) and shortlist ~15.
3. **First touch** (template A): short, personalized, one specific reference to
   their content. No brief attached, no banner, no terms. Goal: a reply.
4. **Brief** (template B + banner): sent only after a reply. Contains name,
   description, links, mechanics, offer shape without numbers.
5. **Call or voice chat:** numbers only here. Agree deliverables per wave,
   disclosure, force-stake mechanics, content co-creation (their voice, our
   facts; we correct mechanics errors only).
6. **Simple written agreement** (even a DM recap): deliverables, wave timing,
   #ad clause, terms-confidentiality, wallet address.
7. **Onboard:** wallet whitelisted, referral link issued from the Ashen Cup
   funnel. **Their referral link is their scoreboard**: arrivals and quest
   participation are already tracked by our own funnel, so KOL performance is
   measurable on our infrastructure, no third-party tools.
8. **Activate by wave** (see content-plan.md): Wave 1 educators at L−16,
   Wave 2 conviction at L−10, Wave 3 countdown at L−3, Wave 4 post-launch
   authentic ("here is my Acolyte", the Forge-gift partners climbing to Pyre).
9. **Post-launch retention:** monthly check-in, early access to feature drops,
   their climb to Immolation as recurring content.
