# Designer Brief — Manifesto Launch Graphic
**Deliverables:** 2 files — one static image, one short video

---

## Context

The manifesto thread is PYRE's first public post on X. The graphic appears only on tweet 1 — it's the first thing anyone sees in their feed before deciding to stop and read. Its only job is to stop the scroll and create a feeling. It does not explain PYRE. No mechanics, no yield numbers, no bullet points.

---

## The Line

> **Stake to survive.**
> **Burn to transcend.**

This is the only text on the graphic. Nothing else.

---

## Visual Direction

**Background:** Near black — `#080504` (PYRE brand background). Full bleed.

**Text:**
- Font: Cormorant Garamond, light weight (300)
- Colour: `#C9A96E` (PYRE gold)
- Wide letter spacing — elegant, not cramped
- Two lines, centred, generous space between them
- "Stake to survive." sits above. "Burn to transcend." sits below. A beat of space between them — they should feel like two separate declarations, not one sentence.

**Fire element:** One controlled, elegant fire element. Not a raging bonfire — something refined. Options:
- A single thin vertical flame, centred below the text
- Rising embers drifting upward from the bottom edge
- A subtle glow emanating from below, as if something is burning underneath the frame

The fire should feel restrained and intentional. Luxury, not spectacle. Think ember, not explosion.

**PYRE wordmark:** Bottom centre, very small, same gold colour, wide letter spacing. Almost invisible — it's a signature, not a logo stamp.

**Nothing else.** No website URL. No "launching June 23." No token ticker. No partnership logos. No borders or frames. The restraint is the point — every other crypto launch graphic is cluttered. This one isn't.

---

## Deliverable 1 — Static Image

- Format: PNG or JPG
- Dimensions: 1600 × 900px (landscape, optimised for X feed)
- The complete composition as described above, static

---

## Deliverable 2 — Short Video (3–4 seconds, silent)

Same visual as the static, but the two lines appear sequentially:

1. Black screen with fire element already present (subtle, slow)
2. "Stake to survive." fades in — holds for 1.5 seconds
3. "Burn to transcend." fades in beneath it — holds for 1.5 seconds
4. Both lines remain. Video ends.

- Format: MP4
- Dimensions: 1600 × 900px
- Silent — no audio
- No hard cut at the end — the final frame should feel like it could hold forever
- Loop point: clean (last frame matches first frame so X autoplay loops seamlessly)

---

## Reference Feel

The aesthetic should feel closer to a luxury fashion brand or a high-end editorial than a crypto project. Dark, quiet, confident. If it looks like a typical DeFi launch graphic it's wrong. If someone could mistake it for a perfume ad or an art exhibition announcement, it's right.

**Brand colours for reference:**
- Background: `#080504`
- Gold accent: `#C9A96E`
- Off-white text: `#EDE8E0`
- Secondary: `#B8A88A`

**Typography for reference:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts, free)
- The two lines should be in this font, light weight, not bold
