# Marketing Assets

The pre-launch funnel runs entirely on our own Village World site: users complete tasks, submit a wallet, and earn a yield boost at launch. KOLs on X drive the traffic. These assets make that machine run.

## The shareable card (high priority)

After completing the funnel, users hit a completion screen with one button — it copies a branded image and opens a pre-filled tweet. This image is our single most important marketing asset: it will sit on hundreds of timelines.

Requirements (locked):

- Dark card, PYRE fire aesthetic, brand gold/ember on near-black.
- **Minimal text** — the image carries the message, not the caption.
- Personalised with the user's **partially shown wallet address** (e.g. `0x4f2…a91c`).
- Tagline area for *"The fire starts [date]."* — date drops in once launch is locked.
- Must feel like a **flex, not a badge** — something people genuinely want on their timeline.
- Template format we can programmatically stamp the wallet address onto.

## X presence

- Profile avatar (logo mark on dark)
- Banner — brand statement, no date dependency
- Post templates: announcement card, mechanic-explainer card ("what is decay?"), countdown card (date-flexible), KOL-shareable cards

## In-funnel art

- Quest/task icons in the village style
- Completion-state celebration moment
- "The fire starts soon" hold screen for pre-reveal

## Notes

- No launch date exists yet — design everything **date-agnostic** with a clean slot where the date will land.
- KOL posts are contractually disclosed (#ad); nothing in the art should imply organic hype that isn't.
